# Overview

This repository hosts an MDX-based documentation and courseware site (hosted on GitHub Pages) designed specifically for travel and club soccer coaches training youth goalkeepers. 

Coaches visit this site at the beginning of their season to gain a comprehensive understanding of:
- What is expected of goalkeepers in their specific age group
- How to evaluate goalies objectively during tryouts and mid-season
- Coaching tips, technical cues, and pedagogy for the position
- Drills specific to goalkeeper training
- How to seamlessly incorporate goalkeepers into team-wide drills and small-sided games

# Sections & Curriculum

We provide distinct sections tailored to the developmental stages of players:

- **6–9 Years (Foundational / Introduction)**
- **9–12 Years (Developmental / Core Technical Mastery)**
- **12–14 Years (Advanced / Tactical Integration)**

# Age Group Module Structure

Each age group section contains the following standardized subsections:

## 1. Intro & Age Considerations
- **Physical & Motor Skill Development:** Aligning physical drills (agility, hand-eye coordination, balance) with biological development across age groups, accounting for growth spurts in older youth.
- **Cognitive Maturity & Tactical Understanding:** Matching tactical complexity to cognitive readiness (from present-moment tracking in U6–U9 to angle and spatial awareness in U12–U14).
- **Psychological Resilience & Error Management:** Building emotional safety nets and resilience to help players handle high-visibility mistakes and bounce back from goals conceded.
- **Multi-Position Rotation vs. Early Specialization:** Guidelines on preventing burnout and overuse injuries by balancing rotational field play with position-specific training.
- **Managing Competitive Pressure & Parent Expectations:** Shifting focus from win-loss records and clean sheets to individual technical growth and decision-making.
- **Time Commitments:** Expectations and impact on players and their families (training hours, equipment).
- **Playing Time Guidelines:** Whole vs. half game recommendations for developmental success.

# Basics of Goalkeeping (The Reference Library)

A foundational reference guide for all coaches covering core technique, mechanics, and terminology:

## 1. Ready/Set Position
- Stance width, weight distribution on balls of feet, knee bend, hand positioning, and eye level.

## 2. Handling
- **Contour Catch (W-Catch):** Forming a 'W' with thumbs and index fingers behind the ball to absorb hard shots securely against the chest.
- **Basket Catch:** Cushioning chest-high balls against the forearms and torso (ideal for younger players developing hand-eye coordination).
- **Scoop Catch (Ground Ball):** Kneeling behind low shots with pinkies touching and arms extended to form a ramp, preventing balls from slipping through.
- **High Contour Catch:** Reaching up with elbows relaxed to secure high balls above the head with soft hands.
- **Side Contour Catch:** Catching shoulder-height balls sideways with one hand behind and one hand on top.
- **Collapse & Contour:** Dropping to the side while maintaining a contour grip on low driven shots.

## 3. Footwork
- **Shuffle:** Rapid lateral movement keeping feet shoulder-width apart without crossing legs.
- **Mini Shuffle:** Small, quick adjustments to fine-tune positioning on the ball line.
- **Backpedal:** Moving backward efficiently while keeping eyes on the ball and torso balanced.
- **Side Running:** Transitioning into running sideways to cover distance across the goal line quickly.
- **Drop Step:** Opening the hips instantly to track a ball played wide or over the top.
- **Back Running:** Sprinting backward while tracking an aerial ball over the head.
- **Cross-over Step:** Power step used to cover large lateral distances rapidly.

## 4. Communication
- **Key Words / Phrases:** *"Keeper's", "Away", "Set", "Push", "Mark Up"*
- **Tone & Delivery:** Concise, Specific, and Loud.

## 5. Coaching Cues & Trigger Words
- **'Set':** Freeze in ready stance as ball carrier prepares to strike.
- **'Step':** Attack the ball on crosses and breakaways.
- **'Drive':** Push off the near foot explosively into dives.

## 6. Distribution
- **Bowl / Underhand Roll:** Rolling the ball smoothly along the turf to a nearby teammate's feet for quick, secure possession.
- **Sling Throw:** Sidearm throwing motion for flat, fast distribution to wide midfielders without bouncing.
- **Baseball Throw / Overhand Release:** Overhand throwing motion used for generating distance downfield to breaking forwards.
- **Punt & Drop Kick:** Striking the ball out of the hands for maximum distance to relieve deep defensive pressure.

## 7. Box Positioning & Angles
- **Ball Line:** Staying square to the shooter on the centerline of the goal.
- **Angle Play:** Cutting down the shooter's window.
- **Follow Defense Out:** Adjusting depth as the defensive line shifts.
- **Recovering:** Recovery runs when caught off line.

## 8. 1v1 Breakaways & Smothering
- **Closing Down:** Quick, aggressive movement off the line to reduce the shooter's reaction window.
- **K-Block / Star Block:** Body shape mechanics for close-range reaction saves.
- **Sliding Smother:** Sliding feet-first or body-wrapping technique to secure the ball at an attacker's feet.

## 9. Drop Passes & Backpass Integration
- **Receiving & Scanning:** Checking shoulders before receiving a backpass from defenders.
- **Opening Hips:** Body orientation to direct the first touch away from incoming pressure.
- **Short Passing & Resets:** Cleanly distributing to center-backs or fullbacks under pressure.

## 10. Aerial Command & High Balls
- **Timing & Takeoff:** Explosive jump driving off one leg with the opposite knee raised for protection.
- **Apex Catch:** Claiming the ball at the highest point with a secure contour grip.
- **Punching:** Fist mechanics (flat surface, thumbs locked) for clearing contested high balls out of the penalty box.

## 11. Base Line Scoring Situations
- **Near Post Defense:** Closing down the tight-angle shot.
- **Ground Crosses:** Smothering and cutting out low passes across the face of goal.

## 12. Outside Box Scoring Situations
- **Cutting Angles:** Managing distance on long-range strikes.
- **Sight Lines:** Positioning to see around defenders.
- **Deflections & Parrying:** Directing second balls away from danger zones.
