# Basics of Goalkeeping (Reference Library)

---

# 1. Ready/Set Position & Handling

### Ready/Set Position
- **Stance Width:** Slightly wider than shoulder-width.
- **Weight Distribution:** Balls of the feet, knees flexed.
- **Hands & Eyes:** Open and ready at mid-chest height; head level tracking the ball.

### Handling Techniques
- **Contour Catch (W-Catch):** Thumbs and index fingers form a 'W' behind the ball.
- **Basket Catch:** Cushion chest-high balls against forearms and torso.
- **Scoop Catch:** Kneeling behind low ground balls with pinkies touching.
- **High / Side Contour:** Soft hands securing aerial and shoulder-height balls.

---

# 2. Footwork & Communication

### Footwork Mechanics
- **Shuffle & Mini Shuffle:** Rapid lateral movement without crossing legs.
- **Drop Step & Backpedal:** Opening hips instantly to track balls played wide or over top.
- **Cross-over Step:** Power step to cover large lateral distances quickly.

### Communication & Cues
- **Key Words:** *"Keeper's", "Away", "Set", "Push", "Mark Up"*
- **Tone & Delivery:** Concise, specific, and loud.
- **Trigger Words:** `'Set'` (freeze in ready stance), `'Step'` (attack crosses), `'Drive'` (explosive push off near foot).

---

# 3. Distribution & Box Positioning

### Distribution Methods
- **Bowl / Underhand Roll:** Smooth turf roll for quick, secure possession.
- **Sling Throw:** Sidearm flat throw for wide distribution without bouncing.
- **Baseball Throw:** Overhand release for deep downfield distance.
- **Punt & Drop Kick:** Maximum distance out of hands to relieve pressure.

### Box Positioning & Angles
- **Ball Line & Angle Play:** Staying square on the centerline and cutting down shooting angles.
- **Follow Defense Out & Recovery:** Adjusting depth as defensive line shifts.

---

# 4. 1v1s, Backpasses & Aerial Command

### 1v1 Breakaways & Smothering
- **Closing Down:** Aggressive movement off line to reduce reaction window.
- **K-Block / Star Block:** Expanding body surface area for close-range saves.

### Backpass Integration & Aerial
- **Scanning & Opening Hips:** Checking shoulders before receiving and orienting touch away from pressure.
- **Apex Catch & Punching:** Claiming at highest point or locking fists to punch to safety.
