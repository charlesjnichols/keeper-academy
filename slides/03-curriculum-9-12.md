# 9–12 Years: Age Considerations & Pedagogy

- **Physical & Motor Skill Development:** Advancing coordination, footwork, and foundational diving mechanics during growth transitions.
- **Cognitive Maturity:** Introducing angle awareness, supporting the defensive line, and basic scanning.
- **Psychological Resilience:** Fostering leadership, positive communication, and accountability.
- **Rotation vs Specialization:** Encouraging continued multi-sport/field participation while dedicating structured time to goalkeeping fundamentals.
- **Playing Time:** Consistent developmental playing time and rotational opportunities.
