# 12–14 Years: Age Considerations & Pedagogy

- **Physical & Motor Skill Development:** Handling adolescent growth spurts, dynamic power development, and aerial dominance.
- **Cognitive Maturity:** Advanced spatial awareness, directional distribution, and sweeping behind high defensive lines.
- **Psychological Resilience:** Elite mentality training, emotional composure under high-pressure competitive environments.
- **Specialized Pathway:** Dedicated goalkeeper training with tactical integration into team sessions.
