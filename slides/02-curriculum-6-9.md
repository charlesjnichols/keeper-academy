# 6–9 Years: Age Considerations & Pedagogy

- **Physical & Motor Skill Development:** Aligning physical drills (agility, hand-eye coordination, balance) with biological development.
- **Cognitive Maturity:** Matching tactical complexity to cognitive readiness (present-moment tracking).
- **Psychological Resilience:** Building emotional safety nets to handle mistakes and concede goals without fear.
- **Multi-Position Rotation:** Preventing burnout by balancing rotational field play with position-specific training.
- **Parent Expectations:** Shifting focus from win-loss records to individual technical growth.
- **Playing Time:** Whole vs. half game recommendations for developmental success.
