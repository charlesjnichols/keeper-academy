# Keeper Academy
### Travel & Club Soccer Goalkeeping Curriculum & Reference Library

Coaches' Pre-Season & In-Season Guide for Youth Goalkeepers (Ages 6–14)

---

# Overview & Objectives

Designed specifically for travel and club soccer coaches training youth goalkeepers. 

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-6 text-left">

<div>

### What Coaches Gain:
- **Age-Specific Expectations:** Clear benchmarks for U6 through U14.
- **Objective Evaluation:** Tryout and mid-season grading criteria.
- **Pedagogy & Cues:** Technical cues and coaching methodologies.
- **Drill Integration:** Seamlessly incorporating keepers into team small-sided games.

</div>

<div>

### Core Curriculum Stages:
1. **6–9 Years:** Foundational & Introduction
2. **9–12 Years:** Developmental & Core Technical Mastery
3. **12–14 Years:** Advanced & Tactical Integration

</div>
