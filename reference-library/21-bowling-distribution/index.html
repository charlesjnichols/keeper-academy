Bowling Distribution

Precision low-trajectory rolling pass to initiate short build-up play to nearby defenders.

What It Is & How to Coach It

Section titled “What It Is & How to Coach It”

What It Is: Underhand rolling restart to distribute smoothly along turf without bounce.

The Sequence of Steps

Section titled “The Sequence of Steps”
  1. Cup ball securely in dominant palm with fingers spread underneath.
  2. Step forward with opposite foot toward target, lowering hips.
  3. Swing arm backward in a smooth pendulum arc.
  4. Release ball at turf level so it glides smoothly into teammate’s feet.

Verbal Cues: “Low and smooth” | “Step with opposite foot” | “Follow through”
