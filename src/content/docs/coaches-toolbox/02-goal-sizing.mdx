title: Right-Sized Goals, Balls & Formats

Hands-on resources for integrating goalkeepers into team sessions: right-sized equipment, build-up restart games, keeper stations, the after-goal reset, and a one-page session template. A keeper's frame should roughly fill the goal, never be swallowed by it. Match the environment to the child's body and the game teaches itself. Exact sizes vary by league; use these as your planning baseline.

## Formats & Goal Sizes

**4v4 (U6-U7):** 4 x 6 ft goals, size 3 ball. No real keeper role; everyone rotates.

**7v7 (U8-U10):** 7 x 21 ft goals, size 4 ball. First keeper turns; goal small enough to feel safe.

**9v9 (U11-U12):** 7 x 21 ft goals, size 4 ball. Full technical base; angles and 1v1 begin.

**11v11 (U13+):** 8 x 24 ft goals, size 5 ball. Specialization, aerial command and power.

## Why It Matters

**Right-Sized Keeps Courage:** A 7-year-old in a full-size goal cannot cover the frame and learns helplessness. A keeper who can actually reach the corners stays brave and keeps trying.
