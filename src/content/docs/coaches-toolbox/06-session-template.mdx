A repeatable session skeleton so a grassroots coach can plan a keeper-friendly practice in minutes, no goalkeeper coach required.

## The Session Skeleton

**60 Minutes for 9-14, 45 for 6-9:** Team warm-up with the keeper in the group (10 min) → keeper station or block (10-15 min) → team game where every restart starts from the GK (25-30 min) → wrap-up with one "cool save" highlight (5 min).

## Equipment

**Packed Bag:** Correct-size balls (3 / 4 / 5 by age), foam balls for 6-9, cones, child-sized or pop-up goals, bibs, and water. Gloves are optional before age 10.

## The One-Page Cue Sheet

**Coaching Cheat Sheet:** Print the reference library's "How to Coach It" cues and keep them in your bag: *"make the W"*, *"pinkies together"*, *"stay big"*, *"set on the strike"*, *"knee to heel"*. The right cue beats the longest lecture.
