Young goalkeepers are the first attacker. Simple, safe distribution builds comfort on the ball and starts teaching how keepers spark the attack.

## [Underhand Roll](/reference-library/21-bowling-distribution/)

**Bowl It Low:** Cup the ball in one hand, step forward with the opposite foot, and roll the ball smoothly along the turf into a teammate's feet. Cue *"low and smooth."*

## Short Inside-Foot Pass

**Keep It Simple:** After a catch, a quick short pass to a defender beside or behind the goal starts the build-up without needing a big kick.

## Defer Big Kicks

**No Punts or Sling Throws Yet:** Young arms and legs lack the capability for punting and overhand throws. Force-feeding them builds bad mechanics; rolls and short passes are plenty.

## Build-Up Distribution Sequence

<Steps>
1. Catch or collect the ball.
2. Scan for a nearby teammate.
3. Release quickly.
4. Choose the short option: an underhand roll or short pass.
5. The build-up begins.
</Steps>
