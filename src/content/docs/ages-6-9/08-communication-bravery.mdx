The earliest leadership habits and the confidence to throw a body at the ball are planted here, one simple call and one soft landing at a time.

## Single-Word Calls

**"Keeper!", "Mine!" & "Leave!":** Teach one clear call for owning the ball and a simple "leave!" that lets the defender know the keeper is taking it. These two triggers form the seed of future backline command.

## Step Up With the Line

**Follow the Defense Out:** Learn to move up as the team advances and recover when possession turns over, a simple habit that builds positional sense.

## Learn to Fall Before Diving

**Safe Falling Groundwork:** Practice rolling and tumbling on soft surfaces with foam balls so children learn to land safely before any real dive is ever attempted.

## The Sequence from Falling to Owning

<Steps>
1. Practice with soft balls to build comfort with falling.
2. Develop safe landing habits.
3. Gain the confidence to dive.
4. Call loudly with a confident "Keeper!".
5. Take ownership of the goal area.
</Steps>
