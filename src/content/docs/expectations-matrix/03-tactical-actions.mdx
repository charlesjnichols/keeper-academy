## Short Distribution (6-9)

**Building Confidence:** Emphasize underhand rolls and short passes to build initial comfort on the ball.

## Box Control & Throwing (9-12)

**Dynamic Playmaking:** Introduce sling throws, punting, K-Block shapes, and high balls as the pitch expands.

## Physical Contact Actions (12-14)

**High-Impact Control:** Reserve sliding smothers and aerial punching for 11v11 play where physical collision risks require athletic coordination.
