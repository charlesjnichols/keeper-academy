## Ground Handling Foundation (6-9)

**Safe Mechanics:** Focus on basket catches, ground and low body positioning. Avoid high-impact collapse diving to protect developing joints.

## Diving & Reaction Expansion (9-12)

**9v9 Transition:** Introduce contour hand shapes (W-catch) and structured collapse diving once players learn proper ground absorption.

## Specialization & Power (12-14)

**11v11 Mastery:** Reserve airborne extension dives and high-velocity parrying control for physical maturity at ages 12–14.
