## Early Habits (6-9)

**Basic Presence:** Teach single-word calls ("Keeper!", "Mine!") and stepping up with the backline as possession moves forward.

## Tactical Positioning (9-12)

**Game Intelligence:** Introduce angle play, ball-line positioning, wall setup, and scanning before receiving passes back from defenders.

## Field Command (12-14)

**Full-Field General:** Command the full 11v11 defensive force and manage sweeper-keeper depth behind a high defensive line.
