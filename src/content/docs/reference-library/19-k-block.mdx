A specialized, close-distance blocking shape used to seal off the space between the legs and maximize lower-body surface area during tight 1v1 encounters where diving is impossible.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  When an attacker is within striking distance, reaction time drops to zero. The K-block turns the goalkeeper's body into a solid wall, eliminating gaps and forcing the ball to hit the body.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Balanced Stance:** Maintain a low, balanced set stance as the attacker approaches within two to five yards.
  2. **The Drop:** As the shot is struck, drop the trailing knee directly to the turf behind the lead heel to close the gap entirely.
  3. **Posture & Chest:** Keep the chest tall, puffed out, and square to the ball line. Never lean backward onto the heels.
  4. **Barrier Extension:** Extend both hands diagonally downward and wide to cover the space outside the legs while keeping hands up to protect the face.
</Steps>

---

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these concise phrases during tight-range shooting drills.

    *Knee to heel* → *Chest tall* → *Hands low and wide*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct common blocking breakdowns immediately.

    **Leaning Backward:** Falling backward causes shots to pop up over the shoulders or through the chest. Enforce a tall, forward-leaning chest.
    
    **Leaving a Gap Between Legs:** Failing to drop the knee completely behind the heel allows low shots to pass through. Cue: *Close the gate.*

    **Dropping Hands:** Lowering the hands leaves the upper body exposed to driven shots. Remind them to keep hands active and protective.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Decision Trigger: K-Block vs. Star Spread">
  The K-block is specifically designed for point-blank, low-driven shots where dropping a knee seals the lower corners. If the attacker is driving across the face of goal or lifting the ball, transition immediately into an expansive Star Spread to cover maximum overall height and width.
</Aside>
