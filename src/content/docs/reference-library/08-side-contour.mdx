The Side Contour Catch is the bridge between a static catch and a full-body dive. It is used for shots arriving between waist and head height that require lateral movement, allowing the goalkeeper to remain on their feet if possible.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  This is a "footwork-first" save. If the keeper reaches without moving their feet, they are inherently off-balance. The goal is to get the nose behind the ball before the hands arrive.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **The Power Step:** Take an explosive lateral step toward the ball’s line of flight. The back foot drives the body; the lead foot sets the platform.
  2. **Aggressive Extension:** Shoot both hands out to intercept the ball. The "W" shape must be established *before* the catch is made.
  3. **Angle Adjustment:** Angle the hands to meet the ball's trajectory (if the ball is rising, hands rotate slightly under; if falling, rotate over).
  4. **Absorption & Recovery:** Immediately upon contact, bend the elbows to pull the ball into the chest. This acts as a shock absorber, preventing the ball from bouncing off the hands.
</Steps>

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these phrases to correct form on the fly.

    *Shift and reach* → *Two hands, every time* → *Bring it home*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:**

    **One-Handed Reaches:** If they reach with one hand, they lose control. Demand "two hands to the ball."
    
    **"Statue" Feet:** Reaching without stepping leaves the keeper off-balance. Enforce the power step.

    **Stiff Arms:** If the ball bounces off their hands, they aren't absorbing. Cue "elbows to ribs."
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Decision-Making Trigger">
  The Side Contour Catch is only efficient if the goalkeeper can get their body behind the ball. If the shot is too wide or too fast to move the feet, stop trying to stand—instruct the keeper to transition into a Collapse Dive immediately. Do not prioritize staying on feet at the expense of conceding a rebound.
</Aside>
