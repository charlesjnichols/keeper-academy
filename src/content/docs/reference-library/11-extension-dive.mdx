Explosive lateral power push to reach mid-height and wide shots beyond simple collapse-dive range.

## What It Is & How to Coach It

**What It Is:** An airborne push off the near leg to extend body length toward wide mid-height shots.

## The Sequence of Steps

<Steps>
1. Step diagonally toward the ball trajectory with the near foot.
2. Drive forcefully off the near leg, extending hip, knee, and ankle.
3. Extend both hands together along the ball flight path.
4. Absorb landing sequentially across the outer leg, hip, and side of the torso, never on elbows or stomach.
</Steps>

**Verbal Cues:** *"Power step"* | *"Drive the hip"* | *"Land on your side"*
