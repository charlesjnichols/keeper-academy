title: Foot Position
---

A distilled, easy-to-use reference for the core technical mechanics of goalkeeping, with the verbal cues and common mistakes to correct for each skill. Foot position optimizes body balance, weight distribution, and footwork mechanics for immediate lateral mobility.

## What It Is & How It's Performed

**What It Is:** The stance and footwork alignment that provides dynamic balance for explosive movement.

**How It's Performed:**
- **Base Width:** Feet shoulder-width to slightly wider; balanced foundation without locking hips.
- **Weight Distribution:** Metatarsals (balls of feet) loaded; heels hovering lightly off the turf.
- **Set Timing (Split Step):** Micro-bounce timing as the shooter strikes the ball to load kinetic energy.
- **Toe Alignment:** Toes pointed toward the ball line to enable instant forward/diagonal steps.

## How to Coach It

**Verbal Cues:** *"Light on toes"* | *"Set on the strike"* | *"Push off the back foot"*

**Common Mistakes to Correct:**
- **Flat-Footed / Heel Heavy:** Causes backward falling and delayed lateral response to low corner shots.
- **Stance Too Wide:** Traps you in place, making quick adjustment steps impossible.
- **Feet Pointing Too Far Sideways:** Reduces your ability to split-step forward or diagonally. Point toes toward the ball line.
