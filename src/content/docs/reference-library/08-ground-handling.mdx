---
title: Ground Handling (Scoop and Basket)
---

Securing rolling ground balls and waist-height shots safely using body-barrier techniques.

## Scoop Catch (Ground Balls)

**What It Is:** Technique for low rolling or bouncing shots.

**How It's Performed:** Lower body into a squat or drop trailing knee to turf behind the ball. Spread hands low with pinkies touching, palms facing up, and funnel the ball up into chest lock.

**How to Coach It:** Cue *"Pinkies together"* and *"Body behind the ball."* Ensure feet/legs act as a secondary wall if hands miss.

## Basket Catch (Waist-Chest Shots)

**What It Is:** Securing hard waist-to-chest-height shots directly into the midsection.

**How It's Performed:** Bend slightly at the waist, extend forearms forward parallel with palms up. Cushion the ball into the soft stomach, folding shoulders over top to lock it.

**How to Coach It:** Cue *"Cradle the baby"* and *"Huddle over the ball."* Watch for elbows flaring out wide, which let shots pop free.
