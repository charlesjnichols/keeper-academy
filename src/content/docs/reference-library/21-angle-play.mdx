Positioning that keeps the goalkeeper between the ball and the center of the goal, cutting the shooter's effective target window and maximizing body frame coverage. Mastering angle play turns a full-sized goal into a narrow target.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  Angle play isn't about running out blindly; it's about geometry. By standing on the imaginary line connecting the ball to the center of the goal, the goalkeeper cuts the shooter's angles in half.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Locate & Track:** Identify the ball's position and trace the direct line running from the ball to the center of the goal line.
  2. **Step on the Line:** Step off the goal line along that imaginary path to reduce the shooter's window, moving forward as pressure increases further upfield.
  3. **Maintain Square Posture:** Keep shoulders and hips square to the ball line as it moves, making micro-adjustments rather than lunging out of position.
  4. **Set & Reset:** Maintain a balanced ready stance, resetting your footing after every shift to prepare for a sudden strike or deflection.
</Steps>

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these concise phrases during positional and shooting drills.

    *Stay on the line* → *Cut the angle* → *Step out to meet the ball*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct common positional breakdowns immediately.

    **Hugging the Goal Line:** Staying glued to the goal line gives the shooter the entire net to aim at. Enforce stepping off the line.
    
    **Drifting Off-Center:** Following the ball without adjusting for the center of the goal leaves the back post exposed. Cue: *Anchor to the middle.*

    **Flat-Footed Shifting:** Sliding sideways instead of stepping along the bisection line throws off balance. Enforce controlled diagonal movement.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Managing Depth vs. Distance">
  Teach goalkeepers the sliding scale of depth: when the ball is far away (near the opponent's half), stay near the edge of the 6-yard box. As the ball enters the final third and central scoring zones, step forward progressively to narrow the shooting window without getting caught too far off the line.
</Aside>
