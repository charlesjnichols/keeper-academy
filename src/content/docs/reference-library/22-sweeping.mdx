Acting as an active participant in defense by reading and intercepting through-balls, protecting the space a high defensive line leaves behind it. The goal is to turn potential goal-scoring opportunities into defensive recoveries.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  A sweeper-keeper isn't just about speed off the line; it’s about decision-making. If you aren't 100% sure you can win the ball, retreat and reorganize.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Constant Scanning:** Keep your hips open and body oriented toward the play, scanning between the ball, the attacker, and your own goal.
  2. **The Decision:** If the pass is played, assess: Am I closer? Is the bounce predictable? If no, prioritize retreating to the goal line.
  3. **The Sprint & Clear:** If you commit, spring forward aggressively. Use a clean touch to clear the ball toward the touchline or a safe open space.
  4. **Immediate Recovery:** After the clearance, immediately turn and retreat to goal-side positioning to reset for the next phase of play.
</Steps>

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these to help the keeper process information under pressure.

    *Read it early* → *I'm out! / Stay back!* → *Clear to the wing*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct common sweeping breakdowns immediately.

    **Hesitation:** Starting, stopping, and starting again creates a disaster. If you commit, follow through. If you doubt, retreat early.
    
    **Blind Sweeping:** Charging out without looking at the center-backs causes collisions. *Communication is non-negotiable.*

    **Central Clearances:** Clearing the ball into the middle of the field invites an immediate shot on an open goal. *Always clear to the wide channels.*
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="danger" title="The Abort Protocol">
  Teach the "Abort" trigger: If the ball takes a bad bounce, the attacker is faster, or you get blocked by traffic, stop the run, change direction, and retreat to the goal immediately. Do not slide tackle an attacker in the open field unless absolutely necessary to prevent a goal.
</Aside>
