The foundational stance taken right before an opponent strikes the ball, allowing explosive reaction in any direction. The pre-shot stance prepares the body to jump, dive, or move laterally with maximum efficiency.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  A static ready position leads to delayed reactions. Teach goalkeepers to time their micro-bounce or set step so they are fully loaded precisely as the boot meets the ball.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Base Width:** Set feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, keeping weight balanced and active on the balls of the feet embedded in the turf.
  2. **Posture:** Flex the knees softly and lean the chest slightly forward over the toes to keep the center of gravity low and dynamic.
  3. **Hand Placement:** Position hands at mid-chest height with palms facing forward, thumbs up, and elbows tucked comfortably near the ribs.
  4. **Head & Focus:** Keep the head level and quiet, locking eyes entirely on the ball through the striker's approach.
</Steps>

---

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use brief, memorable phrases during live shooting drills to instill proper habits.

    *Nose over toes* | *Soft knees* | *Show your palms*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct fundamental stance breakdowns immediately to prevent slow reaction times.

    **Flat Feet / Back on Heels:** Fix delayed reactions by requiring light bouncing or a split-step on the strike.
    
    **Hands Behind Hips:** Correct slow high/low handling by forcing hands forward into the ready visual window.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Preventing Fatigue">
  Holding a low ready position for extended periods during prolonged defending can cause leg fatigue. Teach young players to relax their stance during dead-ball moments or build-up play far away from the box, stepping into the full ready position only as play enters the final third.
</Aside>
