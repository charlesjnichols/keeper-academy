---
title: Ready / Set Position
---

The foundational stance taken right before an opponent strikes the ball, allowing explosive reaction in any direction.

## What It Is & How It's Performed

**What It Is:** The pre-shot stance that prepares the body to jump, dive, or move laterally.

**How It's Performed:**
- **Base:** Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, weight balanced on the balls of the feet (in the turf).
- **Posture:** Knees flexed, chest leaning slightly forward over the toes.
- **Hands:** Positioned at mid-chest height, palms facing forward, thumbs up, elbows tucked near the ribs.
- **Head:** Level and quiet, keeping eyes locked on the ball.

## How to Coach It

**Verbal Cues:** *"Nose over toes"* | *"Soft knees"* | *"Show your palms"*

**Common Mistakes to Correct:**
- **Flat Feet / Back on Heels:** Causes delayed reaction times. Have them bounce lightly on their toes.
- **Hands Behind the Hips:** Lengthens reaction time to high/low shots. Remind them to keep hands forward.
- **Standing Too Tall:** Prevents explosive lateral pushes. Force a lower center of gravity.
