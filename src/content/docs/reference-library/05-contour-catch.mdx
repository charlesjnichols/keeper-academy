---
title: Contour Catch (W-Catch)
---

The primary hand shape used for catching shots arriving between chest and head height.

## What It Is & How It's Performed

**What It Is:** A hand configuration designed to stop high-velocity balls from slipping through to the face or body.

**How It's Performed:**
- **Reach:** Extend arms forward to meet the ball in front of the body line.
- **Hand Shape:** Thumbs and index fingers nearly touch to form a clear "W" shape behind the ball.
- **Spread:** Fingers spread wide across the back contour of the ball.
- **Absorption:** Give slightly with the elbows to cushion impact, bringing the ball safely into chest lock.

## How to Coach It

**Verbal Cues:** *"Attack the ball"* | *"Make the W"* | *"Soft hands, strong wrists"*

**Common Mistakes to Correct:**
- **Thumbs Too Far Apart:** The ball slips through the hands into the face or chest. Ensure thumbs nearly touch.
- **Catching on Chest First:** Ball bounces off the hard chest bone. Force hand contact in front of the body line.
- **Rigid Arms:** Hard hands cause rebounds. Coach absorbing speed into flexing elbows.
