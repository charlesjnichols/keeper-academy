High dives represent the apex of aerial shot-stopping. This maneuver requires an explosive vertical and diagonal airborne launch to parry or catch shots aimed at the upper corners.

<Aside type="caution" title="Safety First">
  This is a high-impact, advanced skill. Never force a goalkeeper into full-power high dives before they have mastered controlled landing mechanics from lower-height extensions.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Positioning:** Execute quick crossover or drop steps to align the body directly under the ball's projected path.
  2. **Explosive Takeoff:** Plant and drive explosively off the jumping leg while driving the non-takeoff knee forcefully upward to maximize vertical lift.
  3. **Reach & Contact:** 
     - **To Catch:** Use both hands in a "W" shape to secure the ball.
     - **To Parry:** Use the top hand to deflect the ball over the bar or wide of the post.
  4. **Absorption:** Cushion the descent onto the lateral torso with arms folded safely across the body to protect the midsection.
</Steps>

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these cues to prioritize height and hand choice.

    *Explode off the leg* → *Drive the knee* → *Top hand to the sky*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct common airborne breakdowns immediately.

    **Trying to Catch Everything:** If the shot is high-velocity, catching can lead to dangerous spills. Teach them to parry over the bar if they cannot firmly "W-catch."
    
    **Landing on Stomach/Elbows:** Landing flat causes breath-taking impact. Enforce a rolling landing across the side of the hip/thigh.

    **Lack of Knee Drive:** Without the knee drive, the goalkeeper loses critical inches of vertical reach. Enforce *Drive the knee*.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip" title="Progression Path">
  Do not jump straight to high-corner shots. Progression should look like this: 
  1. Static "jump-to-catch" (no dive) to master the knee drive. 
  2. Short-distance extension dives on soft mats. 
  3. Full-power high-corner shot stopping.
</Aside>
