Precision low-trajectory rolling pass to initiate short build-up play to nearby defenders. Bowling is the safest and most accurate method for distributing the ball along the turf without bouncing, making it easy for teammates to control under pressure.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coaching Philosophy">
  Distribution is the start of the next attack. A well-placed, bounce-free bowl allows your center-back to receive the ball on the half-turn with maximum composure.
</Aside>

## Mechanical Breakdown

<Steps>
  1. **Secure Grip:** Cup the ball securely in your dominant palm with fingers spread wide underneath to maintain complete control.
  2. **Stance & Step:** Step forward cleanly with the opposite foot toward your intended target while lowering your hips.
  3. **Pendulum Swing:** Swing your arm backward in a smooth, fluid pendulum arc, keeping the arm close to the body.
  4. **Release & Follow Through:** Release the ball smoothly right at turf level so it glides across the grass without bouncing into the teammate's feet.
</Steps>

## Coaching Framework

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Verbal Cues" icon="pencil">
    **Instant Field Triggers:** Use these concise phrases during distribution and build-up drills.

    *Low and smooth* → *Step with opposite foot* → *Follow through to target*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Common Corrections" icon="warning">
    **Targeted Fixes:** Correct fundamental distribution breakdowns immediately.

    **Bouncing the Ball:** Releasing the ball too high causes it to bounce awkwardly, making it difficult for the receiver to control. Enforce turf-level release.
    
    **Stiff Arm Motion:** Punching or pushing the ball stiffly limits accuracy. Cue a relaxed, pendulum-like arm swing.

    **Rushing the Release:** Throwing blindly before teammates are set leads to turnovers. Require a visual scan before the roll.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Scanning & Tactical Integration">
  Before executing a bowl, the goalkeeper must scan the field to ensure the passing lane is open and defenders have split wide to receive. Under modern build-up rules, center-backs can receive inside the 6-yard box; teach your keepers to utilize short, crisp bowls to relieve immediate pressure.
</Aside>
