A short keeper block inside the team session, then straight back into a game. Every activity starts from a catch and ends with a distribution, so habits stay connected to play.

## W-Catch Pairs

**Make the W:** Partners 8 yards apart. Feed firm, chest-to-head balls; the keeper steps to meet it, calls *"Keeper!"*, catches with a W-shape, and brings it to the chest lock. 4 minutes.

## Collapse Dive Foam

**Step, Collapse, Slide:** Low side feeds onto soft ground. Lead step, collapse to the outer thigh, hands behind the ball. Land on the side, never on elbows. 4 minutes.

## 1v1 Gate Game

**Stay Big, Stay Late:** Two goals, two keepers. One ball, one attacker each side; the keeper delays, selects the moment, and spreads the block. 4 minutes, then swap. Finish the block with a bowl or sling to a teammate to start the next game.

## The Keeper Block Sequence

<Steps>
1. **W-Catch pairs** with a teammate at medium distance.
2. **Collapse dive foam** onto soft ground, landing on your side.
3. **1v1 gate game** to apply the same skills under pressure.
4. Finish a bowl or sling, then return to the team game.
</Steps>
