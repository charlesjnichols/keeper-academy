A game-realistic block that keeps the keeper connected to the team: every drill starts from a restart and carries out a sequence under realistic pressure.

## Deflection & Second Shot

**Reset to Feet:** A striker shoots, a coach deflects it with a rebound, the keeper parries to a safe zone and returns to feet for the follow-up shot. 6 minutes.

## Build-Up From the GK

**Short or Long:** 5v4 players and a goalkeeper. Every restart begins from the GK, choosing short to the backline or long to the forward based on the space and press. A back pass to the GK counts double. 7 minutes.

## Crosses Under Traffic

**Claim or Punch:** Serve corners and wide crosses with two attackers challenging the near post. The keeper decides claim vs. punch, calls the attacker *"early"*, and resets to organize. 7 minutes.

## The Keeper Block Sequence

<Steps>
1. **Deflection & second shot** requires resets to feet.
2. **Build-up from the GK** starts every restart from the keeper's feet.
3. **Crosses under traffic** keep the keeper claiming or punching.
4. Finish the block with a game.
</Steps>
